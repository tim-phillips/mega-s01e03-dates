5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

Fixing the parser will not close #4471. The hint row and the one-day moves keep coming in after it ships.

The causes

parser: day and month swapped. The shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant that writes the day first books 7 April and gets an invite for 4 July. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so this is the bulk of the ticket, through the app, email and the booking-v2 form.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY under it. People type what the hint says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The swap looks the same as parser, but it comes only through the form, only at booking-v3 tenants, and fixing the parser leaves it as it is. The fix is the hint (or reading the date the way the hint asks for it).

One-day move: the date moves one day early (#3). Nothing is swapped: Tuesday 12 May arrives as Monday 11 May, and a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday while one in Toronto gets Monday. That reads like a timezone conversion at midnight, not a date format. The desk reads these by hand and lists them in desk-notes.json. The table counts them under parser, by tenant, and the note under it says how many; fixing the parser will not fix them.

Why one ticket held them all

Triage merges on the subject line (decision 0001), and all three causes come in under the same subjects: “Month and day swapped?”, “Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”. Matching subjects never looked at the body, the tenant or the form, which is where the causes differ.

Intake’s tag does not split them either: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the “tagged as” column above shows it disagreeing with the tenant in every row.

What the desk does now

  • New date reports go to the ticket of their cause, by tenant and channel (causes.ts), not by subject.
  • A report whose date lands a day off goes on desk-notes.json and belongs to #3, wherever the table files it.
  • #4471 stays the parser ticket. It closes when the parser fix ships and its row stops growing.
